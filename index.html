Source: RFM Section 5.1.A1, Figure 11b (MAKILA 2A1), rev 11-51 — Twin-Engine Rate of Climb at Vy. Re-digitized 2026-09-23 from the scan embedded in the RFM (313 ppi): the torque-limited lines for every 1,000 ft from −2,000 to 11,000 ft, the hot-day (MCP-limited) curves up to 20,000 ft, and 20 Weight Index curves from 6.5 to 16. Checked against six hand-traced chart points, all within 5 ft/min.

Weight Index comes from RFM Section 5.1.A1, Figure 9 (MAKILA 2A1), rev 11-51 — the version for aircraft without mod 07.26632, whose floor is Hσ = −2,000 ft. Figure 9 is an exact standard-atmosphere relation, WI = weight ÷ 1,000 ÷ σ, and is computed that way here rather than read off the chart.

Still air only. The chart's third panel, which turns rate of climb and headwind into climb gradient (% slope), is not included — wind changes the gradient over the ground, not the rate of climb. Where the chart has no data the tool says which edge you have reached; it never guesses or extrapolates. Rate of climb above 2,000 ft/min is off the top of the chart's scale and is reported as such.
